    How to play

    • Enemies march in from the right along four lanes. Your two tesla towers zap them at random while they are in range.
    • Cash arrives every second, up to your deposit limit. Spend it on four tower upgrades and three defenses.
    • The shield destroys anything that reaches it, but it weakens with every hit. Repair it, or buy an emergency shield for 15 seconds of protection.
    • If the shield is gone and an enemy reaches your base, the game is over. Your score is how long you lasted.
    • Keys: 1 to 7 buy items. P or Esc pauses, and the pause menu lets you quit.
