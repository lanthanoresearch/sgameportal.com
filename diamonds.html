How to Play

The idea

  • Your ball bounces up and down on its own so you steer it left and right.
  • The ball only breaks blocks of its own color. Any other color just bounces it away.
  • You start white. Hit a striped color change block to switch to its color.
  • Black blocks are deadly. Touch one and you use a spare ball and the ball turns white again. Every level has one, so you can always reset your color.
  • Break every colored block to clear the level.
  • A pink reverse block breaks with any color, but then left and right swap until the next level.
  • A gravity block with an arrow breaks with any color. The ball then falls or rises and bounces the same height off whatever it lands on. While gravity is on, every other gravity block glows with a double arrow, and touching any of them switches gravity off.
  • A silver block never breaks. The ball just bounces off it.
  • White blocks can only be broken by a white ball, so clear them first. Dying turns the ball white again.

Scoring

  • 1 point per block.
  • The Bonus starts at 360 and drains while you play. Whatever is left is added to your score when you clear the level.
  • You earn an extra ball every 250 points.
  • You start with 4 spare balls, shown at the top. Die with none left and it is Game Over.
  • Make the top ten and put your name in the record books.

Keyboard

← → / A DSteer the ball. The game waits for your first press, so it never starts by accident. P / EscPause

Touch

  • Buttons: hold the left or right button to steer.
  • Gesture: touch anywhere and drag left or right. Drag the other way to change direction.
  • The game waits until you pick a direction, so it never starts by accident.
  • Use the pause button next to the score.
